About Us

Scenic City Robotics (Team 12474) is a rookie FRC team that meets at Chattanooga State Community College in Chattanooga, Tennessee. FRC challenges teams to design, build, and program a full-size robot to play a new game each year.

Our Mission

To inspire students to pursue STEM through teamwork, creativity, and real-world engineering experience.
